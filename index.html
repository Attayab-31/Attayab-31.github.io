Featured Research: AgentQuotaBench

Evaluating Multi-Agent LLM Workflows Under Shared Inference Quotas
Research Protocol, Version 1.1 · Published November 16, 2026

A benchmark protocol for studying capacity-aware admission control, deadline-adjusted goodput, and tenant fairness in multi-agent LLM workflows.

View the Zenodo publication

Overview

AgentQuotaBench investigates how multi-agent LLM workflows behave when concurrent user workflows compete for a shared inference-capacity pool. Parallel agents can help complete individual tasks, while also concentrating demand on shared inference resources.

The protocol proposes a controlled comparison of four workflow architectures under matched task and resource-accounting rules. It studies whether quota-aware admission can improve service-level goodput, deadline attainment, and fairness without degrading task quality.

Research question

When many user workflows share a finite inference quota, which workflow architecture delivers the most correct, on-time completions per unit of shared capacity, and can quota-aware admission improve service-level goodput without degrading task quality?

Experimental conditions

IDWorkflowRole
S1Single-agentBaseline: one agent completes the task.
S2Independent best-of-threeThree independent attempts use isolated task states and share a per-workflow resource ceiling.
M2Planner-executor-verifierA planner creates a bounded task graph; up to two workers execute independent leaves, and a verifier checks the final state.
M3Quota-aware M2The M2 topology with atomic capacity reservation before worker dispatch; it can wait, reduce worker count, or fall back to sequential execution.

Workloads and conditions

DimensionPrespecified design
WorkloadsAppWorld API and tool-use tasks; a frozen Terminal-Bench release
Task selectionAt least 60 unique tasks per workload, subject to licensing and reproducibility checks
Concurrency1, 4, 8, and 16 logical workflows
ArrivalsSteady open-loop and burst
Quota profilesLow, medium, and high, normalized to measured single-agent demand
ReplicationThree independent execution seeds

Quota profiles are emulator settings, not claims about any provider's commercial plan or published quota tier.

Evaluation

The primary endpoint is deadline-adjusted goodput: grader-passing workflows completed before their frozen deadlines per minute of a fixed open-loop measurement window. Task success and p95 end-to-end latency are prespecified guardrails. Secondary measures include queue delay, rate-limit responses, retry amplification, token use, cost per successful task, coordination overhead, and tenant fairness.

The separate mixed-tenant fairness track includes Jain's fairness index, absolute goodput, per-tenant service rates, worst-tenant outcomes, and starvation events.

Planned scale

34,560 core confirmatory workflow episodes: 60 tasks × 2 workloads × 4 architectures × 4 concurrency levels × 2 arrival profiles × 3 quota profiles × 3 independent seeds. The mixed-tenant fairness track is additional.

This is a research protocol and benchmark design, not a completed empirical evaluation. No benchmark outcome values or confirmatory findings are claimed.
